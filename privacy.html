What we collect, why, and how to ask us to forget you.

Privacy policy.

Plain English. No tricks. We don't sell your data, we don't track you across the web, and we don't pretend cookies (the digital kind) are a security feature.

Last updated · 29 April 2026

The TL;DR. We collect your name, address, email and phone when you order or sign up. We use it to bake and deliver your tin, send you the occasional newsletter (only if you ask), and keep our books in order for HMRC. We share it with the courier who's delivering your tin and the payment processor who took your money — that's it. We don't sell it, rent it, or feed it to advertising algorithms.

You can ask us to show you what we have, fix it, or delete it any time. hello@mjolk.co.uk.

Who we are.

Mjölk Cookies Co. Ltd ("Mjölk", "we", "us") is a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 14839201), with its registered office at Floodgate Studios, Floodgate Street, Digbeth, Birmingham B5 5SL. We are the data controller for the personal data described below, and we're registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

What we collect.

Depending on what you do on the site, we collect:

  • Order details — your name, billing address, email, phone, the recipient's name and delivery address (for gifts), and the contents of your tin.
  • Payment information — handled by Stripe. Your card number never touches our servers; we only see the last four digits and a transaction ID.
  • Account details — for subscribers: a password hash, delivery preferences, and your subscription history.
  • Newsletter — if you sign up: just your email address and the date you subscribed.
  • Contact form messages — whatever you write to us, plus the email or phone number you gave so we can reply.
  • Postcode lookups — when you check delivery, the postcode is processed in your browser only; we don't store it server-side.
  • Server logs — our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention.

Why we collect it.

Under UK GDPR, we need a lawful basis for every bit of processing. Ours are:

  • Contract — to bake your cookies, take payment, hand the tin to a courier, and send you order updates. We can't do any of that without the basics.
  • Legal obligation — to keep accounting records (HMRC requires 7 years), comply with food-safety law, and respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • Legitimate interest — to prevent fraud, fix bugs, improve the site, and write to you about an order issue. We balance this against your privacy and don't use it as a catch-all.
  • Consent — for the newsletter, any optional marketing, and analytics if we ever add any. You can withdraw consent any time without affecting earlier processing.

Who we share it with.

A short list of trusted sub-processors, each with their own UK-GDPR commitments:

  • Stripe — to take and refund payments.
  • Royal Mail, DPD, Stuart, Gophr — to deliver your tin. We share the delivery name, address and phone number; they don't see what you ordered.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider. They process server logs (data centres in EU/UK).
  • Google Workspace — for our hello@ and notes@ inboxes. They don't read or use your messages for advertising under our paid plan.
  • Mailchimp (if you've signed up to the newsletter) — to send the email and track whether you opened it. You can opt out any time.
  • HMRC, our accountants, and the ICO — when legally required.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. We don't run ad-tracking pixels. We don't share with social-media platforms unless you've explicitly opted in.

How long we keep it.

  • Order records — 7 years (HMRC requirement).
  • Subscription account data — until you cancel, then 7 years for the payment record, then deleted.
  • Newsletter list — until you unsubscribe, then we delete your email within 30 days.
  • Contact-form messages — 2 years from the last reply, then deleted.
  • Server logs — up to 30 days, then aggregated or deleted.

Your rights.

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (a "subject access request").
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data erased ("the right to be forgotten") — subject to our legal retention obligations above.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Have your data transferred to another service in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent for newsletter or optional marketing at any time.
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you think we've got it wrong. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first, though.

Email hello@mjolk.co.uk with the subject line "Privacy request" and we'll reply within 30 days, usually a lot sooner. We may need to verify your identity before sending you data — usually by replying from the email address on the order.

Cookies and storage.

The site uses local storage (so your tin remembers what you put in it between pages) and a small number of essential cookies set by our hosting provider. We don't run advertising or analytics cookies. Full details on the Cookie policy page.

International transfers.

Most of your data stays in the UK or EU. A handful of our sub-processors (Stripe, Mailchimp) may transfer data to the US under the UK Data Bridge or Standard Contractual Clauses, both of which provide an adequate level of protection under UK GDPR.

Updates to this policy.

If we change anything material, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top and — for changes that affect your rights — email everyone on the newsletter and anyone with an active subscription. Minor edits (typos, restructuring) we'll just quietly fix.
