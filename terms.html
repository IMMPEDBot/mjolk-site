Terms & conditions.

These set out the deal between you and us when you use the site or buy a tin. We've kept them as short and as plain as we could without making our solicitor nervous.

Last updated · 29 April 2026

The TL;DR. You order a tin, we bake it and send it. Pay when you order. We deliver same-day in Birmingham (B-postcodes), next-working-day across the UK. If you don't love them, tell us within 7 days and we'll send a replacement or refund. We can't promise life-changing cookies, but we'll try.

Ltd ("Mjölk", "we", "us") is a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 14839201, VAT GB 423 891 776), with its registered office at Floodgate Studios, Floodgate Street, Digbeth, Birmingham B5 5SL. These terms govern your use of mjolk.co.uk and any orders you place with us. By placing an order or signing up to a subscription, you confirm you've read and accepted them.

2. Your order.

You place an order through the website. The contract between us forms when we confirm your order by email (not before — until then, we may decline). We can refuse an order without giving a reason; the most common reason is that we're fully booked for the delivery day you wanted, in which case we'll offer the next available slot or a full refund.

Once an order is confirmed, you have the right to cancel under section 6, with the exceptions noted there.

3. Prices & payment.

  • All prices are in GBP and include VAT at the prevailing rate.
  • Delivery is charged separately at checkout — see our delivery page for the current tier prices.
  • We use Stripe for payment. Your card details are handled by Stripe and don't pass through our servers.
  • Prices on the site are correct at the time you place your order. Obvious pricing errors (e.g. £2 instead of £18) won't be honoured — we'll email and offer a refund or the correct price.

4. Delivery.

Three tiers, summarised below — full detail on the delivery page:

  • Same-day Birmingham (B-postcodes within ~10 miles of Digbeth) — order by 11am Mon–Fri, arrives 4–7pm. £6.95.
  • Next-working-day West Midlands (CV / DY / WS / WV) — order by 2pm Mon–Thu, arrives next working day. £5.95.
  • Next-working-day UK — Royal Mail Tracked 24, order by 2pm Mon–Thu. £5.95.
  • Free on any tier when you spend over £60.

"Warm" only applies to same-day Birmingham, and only if you eat them within an hour of the courier handing them over. UK next-day tins arrive at room temperature with a printed reheating card. We don't guarantee specific time-windows for courier deliveries — couriers occasionally run late, and we won't be liable for short delays beyond our control (see section 11).

Risk in the goods passes to you when the courier hands them to you (or to anyone at your delivery address). If the package arrives obviously damaged, refuse delivery or photograph it before opening, and email us within 48 hours.

5. Allergens.

Our cookies are made in a kitchen that handles wheat, milk, egg, soy and nuts (specifically pistachio, in our Dubai Chocolate cookie). Cross-contact is possible. We can't guarantee any cookie is free from any allergen we use elsewhere. Don't order if you have a severe allergy and can't tolerate cross-contact. Full ingredient lists on the allergens page.

6. Cancellations & the Consumer Contracts Regulations.

Cookies are perishable food, so they're exempt from the standard 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013. We still try to be reasonable:

  • Before we've baked it (typically before 9am on the bake day) — full refund, no questions.
  • After we've baked it but before dispatch — 50% refund. We can't easily resell warm cookies.
  • After dispatch — we can't recall the courier, so the order stands. If something's wrong with what arrived, see section 7.

Email hello@mjolk.co.uk with your order number and we'll process it.

7. The not-loved-it guarantee.

If your tin arrives damaged, late, or just isn't what we promised, email hello@mjolk.co.uk within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We'll either send a replacement tin (free) or refund the order in full. We use this maybe a dozen times a year and we'd genuinely rather you came back than feel stuck. This is in addition to your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

8. Subscriptions.

  • Plans run on a rolling basis — weekly, fortnightly, or monthly.
  • You're charged on the dispatch day of each delivery.
  • You can pause for up to 12 weeks, skip a single delivery (with 24h notice), or cancel any time from your account. No retention call.
  • If we change a subscription price, we'll email at least 30 days before the next charge so you can decide.

9. Gift tins.

For gifts, you'll provide the recipient's address at checkout. We'll deliver there (not to you). Handwritten notes are written by an actual human; we reserve the right to politely decline notes that are abusive, defamatory, or otherwise illegal. We may text the recipient on dispatch if you've supplied their phone number — let us know if you'd rather we didn't.

10. Acceptable use of the site.

Don't try to break into the site, scrape it at scale, run automated checkouts, or do anything that would be a problem if everyone did it. We can suspend or cancel orders made in bad faith.

11. Liability.

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, fraud, or for any other liability that can't be limited by law.

Subject to that: our total liability for any single order is capped at the value of that order. We're not liable for indirect or consequential losses (e.g. you were going to use the cookies to propose, the courier was late, you're now single). We're also not liable for failures caused by events outside our reasonable control — courier strikes, snow, power cuts, illness in a small kitchen — though we'll do our best to refund or reschedule.

12. Changes to these terms.

We may update these terms occasionally. The version that applies to your order is the one published when you place the order — we don't change retroactively. Material changes get an email if you have an active subscription.

13. Governing law.

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have non-exclusive jurisdiction. If any provision turns out to be unenforceable, the rest still applies.
