Who we work with

Three kinds of partners.

Cafés & restaurants

Independent cafés in Moseley, Stirchley, Kings Heath, Harborne and the Jewellery Quarter. Restaurants doing a proper afternoon menu. Anyone serious about coffee who wants a cookie that holds its own next to it.

Currently stocked at: Yorks, Quarter Horse, Saint Kitchen, Faculty.

Hotels & B&Bs

In-room amenity tins, afternoon-tea spreads, conference catering. Typically a smaller branded version of our standard tin or our cookies plated bare for service. Custom tin lid wraps available at volume.

Currently in: The Grand Birmingham, Hotel du Vin, Edgbaston Park Hotel.

Corporate kitchens

Larger offices with internal catering teams that want our cookies plated for client meetings, all-hands, or daily tea-points. Same wholesale pricing. We can supply unbranded for resale or branded for direct service.

For one-off team treats — see our Corporate fika page instead.

What we look for

We supply about 18 venues and aren't trying to grow that quickly. We say no to roughly two-thirds of applicants — usually because the customer fit isn't quite right or volumes are too low. Don't take it personally if we pass; we're trying to keep our existing partners well-supplied.

Marketing support

All trade partners get a small Mjölk display card for the counter, free updates when we have a new flavour, and a feature on our Instagram once a quarter. Larger partners get co-branded options on request.
