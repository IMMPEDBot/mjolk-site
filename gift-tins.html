The handwritten bit

Free with every gift.

At checkout you'll get a small text box and 80 characters. Whatever you write, we write — actually by hand, on a Mjölk postcard, slipped into the tin. We will not autocorrect your in-jokes, will not soften your sentiments, and will absolutely write rude things if asked.

Want longer than 80 characters? Send it to notes@mjolk.co.uk with your order number and we'll handwrite up to a full A6 card. Free.
