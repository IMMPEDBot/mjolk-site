How to warm them up.

straight from Nan's notebook

  1. Oven (best): Pop the tin (lid removed) into a preheated oven at 300°F / 150°C for 5–7 minutes, until soft and fragrant.
  2. Microwave (quickest): Take a cookie out of the tin and onto a microwave-safe plate. Heat for 8–12 seconds. Eat immediately.
  3. Storage: Keeps best at room temperature for up to 2 days. For longer, refrigerate. They'll keep a week.
⚠ Never put the metal tin in the microwave. (Sounds obvious. It happens.)

Need to know

The fine print.

Where do you deliver same-day? +
All Greater London postcodes (anything starting E, EC, N, NW, SE, SW, W, WC). Order before 2pm Mon–Sat and your tin lands by 6pm. Outside London, we ship next-day across the UK via DPD overnight.
Are the cookies actually warm when they arrive? +
Genuinely, yes. Cookies go from the oven into the tin, the tin gets sealed warm, and a courier collects within the hour. Most arrive between 30–55°C inside the tin — gooey territory. If they've gone fully cold, the reheating instructions on this page take 8 seconds.
What allergens are in the cookies? +
All four cookies contain wheat, milk, egg and soy. The Dubai Chocolate cookie also contains pistachio (nuts) and is made in a kitchen that handles nuts, so we can't guarantee no cross-contamination for severe nut allergies. Full ingredient lists are sent with every tin.
How long do they keep? +
Best within 24 hours, warm. Sealed in the tin at room temperature they're great for 2 days, decent for 3. After that, fridge them — they'll keep a week. For longer storage, freeze the cookies (not the tin) and reheat from frozen at 150°C for 8 minutes.
Can I send a tin as a gift? +
Yes — that's most of what we do. Add a recipient address at checkout and a handwritten note on the back of a Nan's Tin postcard (free). The tin itself is meant to be kept, and it makes the gift feel like a gift, not a takeaway.
What if I don't love them? +
Drop us a note within 7 days and we'll either send a replacement tin or refund the order in full. We've used it about a dozen times in two years. We'd rather you came back than feel stuck with a tin you didn't enjoy.
Are the tins reusable? +
Very. Powder-coated steel, food-safe lining, 18cm across. Customers use them for biscuits (obviously), tea bags, sewing kits, dog treats, and — in one memorable case — engagement rings. Wash by hand.
