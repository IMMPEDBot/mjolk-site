Honesty about our kitchen

A note on cross-contamination.

Our Digbeth kitchen handles wheat, milk, egg, soy and tree nuts (pistachio) every working day. We don't run separate production lines or designated allergen-free benches.

We do clean down between batches and we're hot on standard food hygiene practices — ★★★★★ rated by Birmingham City Council Environmental Health, last audited March 2026. But we cannot guarantee a zero-trace environment.

If you have a severe nut, dairy, egg or wheat allergy, please don't risk it. We'd rather lose your order than take a chance with your safety. Coeliacs in particular: our flour is regular T55 wheat, not gluten-free, and the kitchen isn't gluten-free either.

Got questions about a specific allergen, or want a heads-up on which cookies are made on which days? Email hello@mjolk.co.uk and we'll come back the same working day. Genuinely.

Vegan?

Not yet. We've tested four vegan recipes in 2025 and rejected all of them — none reached the standard we want. We'd rather not have a vegan option than a bad one. We'll get there.

Gluten-free?

Also not yet. Our kitchen handles wheat heavily and we don't think a "gluten-free in a contaminated kitchen" claim is honest. Working on a partner GF kitchen for 2027.

Halal?

Yes — none of our cookies contain alcohol or animal-derived gelatine. Our vanilla extract is alcohol-based but gets fully baked off; if that's a deal-breaker for you, please let us know and we'll swap in vanilla paste for your order.

Kosher?

Our ingredients are individually kosher-certified or kosher-by-ingredient, but our kitchen is not kosher-certified. We can't make a kosher claim under that standard, but we're happy to share full sourcing info on request.
