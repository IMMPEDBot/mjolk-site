Pause / skip / swap

No contract. No fuss.

Subscriptions on the internet are mostly a trap — three clicks to start, eleven to cancel. Ours aren't. You log in, you click. We don't make you call. We don't ask why. We don't send a sad-eyed retention email. Promise.

Just in case

Subscription questions.

When does my first tin arrive? +
Subscriptions ship the next available delivery day after sign-up. For Birmingham (B-postcodes) that's usually the next Tuesday — or earlier if you sign up before 11am. UK next-day tiers ship on the next weekday. We'll email you the exact day with your confirmation.
Can I change my delivery day? +
Yes — Tuesday is the default but you can pick any weekday at sign-up or change it later in your account. Same-day Birmingham cutoff is 11am Mon–Fri; UK next-day cutoff is 2pm Mon–Thu.
What about Christmas, Easter, bank holidays? +
We deliver Mon–Fri but close on the UK bank holidays in spring/summer plus a fortnight at Christmas. Subscriptions automatically shift to the next working day, and we always email a heads-up.
Do I get a discount on the first tin? +
The 10% subscriber discount applies from your first tin, so yes. If you've also got a £10 first-tin code from the newsletter, that stacks for the first delivery only.
Can I gift a subscription? +
Yes — pick the recipient's address at checkout and choose a duration (1, 3, 6, or 12 months). They'll get a card with the first tin. We don't auto-renew gifted subscriptions, so they won't be left with a recurring charge.
How do I cancel? +
Log into your account, hit "cancel subscription," that's it. No retention call, no winback discount, no "are you sure?" three times. We'd much rather you came back when you're ready.
