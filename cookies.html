Cookie policy.

Almost ironic, this. We sell cookies (the soft, gooey kind), and we use almost none of the digital ones. Here's exactly what's running.

Last updated · 29 April 2026

The TL;DR. We use a small amount of local storage in your browser to remember what's in your tin between pages. We don't run advertising cookies, analytics cookies, social-media tracking pixels, or anything else that follows you around the internet. Our hosting provider sets a couple of essential cookies for security. That's it.

What's a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to remember. The browser hands it back to the same website on every later visit. Cookies are how a site recognises you've been there before.

"Local storage" is a similar but separate thing — it's data the website saves in your browser that it can read on later visits, but it never gets sent over the network on every request the way cookies do. It's slightly more private by design.

What we set on this site.

Local storage (browser only)

  • mjolk_cart_v1 — remembers the cookies you've added to your tin so the cart isn't empty when you flick between the homepage, gift tins, and the cart drawer. Stays in your browser until you clear it. Never sent to our servers.

Essential cookies (security & hosting)

  • __cf_bm (set by our hosting/CDN provider, Cloudflare via Vercel) — bot-detection cookie that distinguishes humans from automated traffic. Lifetime: 30 minutes after your last request. Required for the site to be online.
  • __vercel_* — short-lived session cookies our hosting platform uses for routing and request integrity. Lifetime: session.

What we don't use

  • No Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, or any other site-analytics tool.
  • No advertising cookies (no Google Ads, no Meta Pixel, no TikTok pixel).
  • No social-share trackers (no Facebook Like, no embedded tweets).
  • No remarketing or retargeting.
  • No A/B testing platforms.

If we ever add light analytics, it'll be cookieless (the kind that aggregates request logs without identifying you), and we'll update this page with full detail before we turn it on.

Third-party services.

  • Google Fonts — we serve Fraunces, Newsreader and Caveat from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may log the IP address that requests the font file. We don't get any of that data, and Google's terms restrict its use to operating Google Fonts. If you'd rather we self-hosted the fonts, email us.
  • Stripe (only at checkout, when we add payments) — sets its own cookies for fraud prevention. Stripe's cookie policy is at stripe.com/cookie-settings.

How to clear them.

Every browser lets you clear cookies and local storage from its settings panel. Quick paths for the common ones:

  • Chrome / Edge — Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → tick "Cookies and other site data".
  • Firefox — Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Safari — Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove (search "mjolk" to clear just ours).
  • iOS Safari — Settings app → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.

Clearing local storage will empty your tin — you'll need to add cookies again next visit. (The edible kind we send fresh, every time.)

Updates to this policy.

If we add a new cookie or service, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date. Material changes get an email if you have an active subscription.
