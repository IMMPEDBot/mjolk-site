Where we go, and when.

Same-day Birmingham. UK next-working-day.

Three tiers, no surprises. Same-day for Birmingham B-postcodes (within roughly 10 miles of our Digbeth kitchen). Next working day for the rest of the West Midlands and the UK. Free on any tier when you spend over £60.

Three tiers. One Digbeth kitchen.

Same-day delivery is Birmingham only — the operational reality of getting a warm tin to a doorstep within hours. The rest of the West Midlands and the UK get next-working-day on Royal Mail Tracked 24, sealed fresh from this morning's bake.

All other UK · next-working-day

We can't truthfully promise a "warm-to-the-touch" tin past Birmingham — couriers just don't move fast enough. So we don't. Next-day tins arrive at room temperature with a reheating card; three minutes in a low oven and the gooey middle is back.

Outside the West Midlands

UK-wide, overnight.

We can't get a warm tin to Aberystwyth in three hours and we won't pretend we can. What we do instead: bake fresh on dispatch day, vacuum-seal the cookies in food-grade liner inside the keepsake tin, hand it to Royal Mail Tracked 24, and have it on your doormat the next working day. Inside the liner the cookies stay soft for two days.

Reheat at 150°C / 300°F for 3 minutes and you've got the same gooey middle as a same-day tin. Every UK-wide tin ships with a printed reheating card.

A note on the islands

We deliver to the Highlands, Northern Ireland, Isle of Wight, Isle of Man, Channel Islands, and the Scottish Isles. Lead time bumps up to 2–3 working days for these and the cost goes to £14.50, but we get there. Email hello@mjolk.co.uk if you need a specific arrival date.
